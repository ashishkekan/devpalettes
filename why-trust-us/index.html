Privacy & Transparency

You can inspect our source code on GitHub, read our Privacy Policy, or follow the examples in our guides. A generated palette is a starting point, not a guarantee of accessibility or suitability for a brand. The ranking dashboard is a simulator and does not measure Google rankings.
